# Android TV 16 for PS4

Run Android TV on your PlayStation 4. Launch it from the PS4 home screen, then use your DualShock 4 to watch live TV, stream video or play media from your home network.

![Android TV for PS4](branding/repository-banner.svg)

## Features

- Google TV Home with controller navigation. Android apps, accounts and settings stay on the internal drive between launches.
- SmartTube for YouTube, BackWhen TV for live channels, and Plex and Jellyfin for your media server.
- Kodi and VLC for playback. Material Files can browse shared folders on a NAS over SMB.
- Aurora Store for downloading apps, with microG Services for apps that use Google account integration.

## Installation and compatibility

The Android 16 port targets PS4 Fat, Slim and Pro with one installable PKG and one Strawberry kernel. The new package is awaiting public release. Playback has been verified on Aeolia during development. Belize and Baikal still need confirmation with the release package.
